Section 02 · The capstone project

Purpose, question, and scope

Project purpose

Reframing the coach role

The purpose of the capstone was to strengthen how the Black Graduation Coach role is understood, supported, and connected to institutional accountability. It reframes the coach as more than an individual service provider. The coach is a trusted relational anchor, a navigator and pathway guardian, and a source of situated knowledge about how institutional conditions are experienced by Black students and families.

Final capstone question

How can the Black Graduation Coach role support Black students' belonging, agency, achievement, and flourishing while helping the school system identify and transform the institutional conditions producing inequitable pathways?

Objectives

Five working objectives

  • Synthesize scholarship, human-rights analysis, local strategy, and comparative public practice relevant to Black student flourishing and Graduation Coaching.
  • Clarify the relational, pathway, community, institutional, and sustainability dimensions of the coach role.
  • Develop an evidence-informed framework and practical implementation prototypes.
  • Specify accountability and evaluation questions that measure institutional response, not only student outcomes.
  • Demonstrate growth across the five Health Equity & Sustainability competencies.

The applied contribution

What the capstone contributes

The project contributes a defensible problem formulation, an integrated five-domain framework, seven clearly articulated coach functions, eight implementation recommendations, practical prototype components, and an evaluation agenda. Its academic value lies in making the constructs, relationships, implementation conditions, and limits explicit enough to support future review, co-development, piloting, and evaluation.

Integrity boundary

Historical Toronto District School Board data and Ontario-wide human-rights findings are used as illustrations of pathway inequity. They are not represented as current TVDSB outcomes. The forthcoming August 2026 conversation with Dr. Carl James occurs after submission and is not part of this evidence base.
