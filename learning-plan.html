Learning outcomes and completion record

The original blank course template has been reconstructed here as a final learning contract. It reflects the work actually completed and the competencies demonstrated, rather than preserving an outdated preliminary plan.

Outcome 1 · Jan–Jul 2026

Analyze Black Graduation Coaching through a health-equity and anti-racism lens

Competencies — Expertise in Health Equity & Sustainability; Critical Reasoning

Action plan — Review critical scholarship, human-rights analysis, and TVDSB strategy; identify structural drivers; reject deficit framing.

Resources — James, hooks, Ani, OHRC, TVDSB strategy, public education research.

Capstone evidenceEvaluation plan
Literature/policy synthesis; problem formulation; findings; health-equity rationale.Evidence accurately distinguishes structural drivers, rights obligations, and local strategy; mentor review and final presentation.

Outcome 2 · Feb–Jul 2026

Apply systems thinking to connect student experience with institutional responsibility

Competencies — Systems Thinking & Applied Expertise; Critical Reasoning

Action plan — Map the relationships among students, families, coaches, guidance, Student Success, leadership, data, policy, and accountability.

Resources — Systems thinking concepts, implementation design, strategy commitments, comparative practices.

Capstone evidenceEvaluation plan
Five-domain framework; theory of change; coach-to-leadership feedback loop; evaluation agenda.Framework specifies actors, mechanisms, implementation conditions, feedback, and measurable institutional response.

Capstone evidenceEvaluation plan

Learning plan assessment

The learning outcomes were substantially addressed. The strongest evidence is the movement from a broad justice concern to an explicit systems model with bounded claims, implementation mechanisms, competency evidence, and an evaluation agenda. The central area requiring future development is empirical testing: co-development with Black students, families, coaches, and leaders; a local baseline; pilot implementation; and mixed-method evaluation.
