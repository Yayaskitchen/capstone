Knowledge mobilization

The portfolio is designed for several audiences without pretending that one product can serve every purpose. The same core argument can be translated differently while retaining its evidence boundaries.

Next-stage agenda

  • Co-develop the framework with Black students, families, community partners, coaches, and institutional leaders.
  • Establish a privacy-protected, disaggregated local baseline.
  • Convert prototype components into a bounded pilot with defined decision rights and resourcing.
  • Evaluate both student flourishing and institutional response using mixed methods.
  • Report findings back to rights-holders in accessible forms and document how their interpretation changes decisions.
  • Use the forthcoming August 2026 conversation with Dr. Carl James to deepen post-submission reflection and knowledge mobilization — not retroactively treated as part of this capstone evidence base.
