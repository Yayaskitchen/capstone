Mentorship, knowledge, and responsibility

Acknowledgements

Dr. Marcy Saxe-Braithwaite is acknowledged for academic leadership and guidance within the APPLHSCI 9099 Capstone Experience.

Colette Greaves, current Field Mentor at the Thames Valley District School Board, is acknowledged for her role in the final phase of mentorship, scope refinement, and contextual learning.

Rheba Moore-Nash, System Principal at the Thames Valley District School Board and original Field Mentor, is acknowledged for helping establish the early direction of the capstone.

The Black students, families, educators, community advocates, scholars, and institutional leaders whose knowledge and struggle have made anti-Black racism in education impossible to treat as an unknown problem are acknowledged with gratitude. Their work gives this project both an evidence base and a responsibility.

Special acknowledgement is owed to the intellectual contributions of Carl James, bell hooks, and Marimba Ani, whose work required this capstone to look beyond access and representation toward pathway, voice, worldview, power, dignity, and institutional transformation.

Black communities have already named the problem. The institutional task is to listen, act, account, and change. Acknowledgement of responsibility

Confidentiality and ethical practice

This portfolio contains no identifiable student information and no confidential TVDSB records. It is grounded in public scholarship, public policy and strategy documents, de-identified professional reflection, and applied design work. Comparative public examples are used only as practice illustrations. The framework and toolkit components are clearly identified as evidence-informed prototypes requiring review, co-development, approval, and testing before organizational use.

Authorship and scope statement

This learning portfolio represents my capstone inquiry, professional learning, synthesis, and reflection. It is submitted for academic assessment in APPLHSCI 9099. It should not be represented as an official TVDSB framework, policy, evaluation, or approved implementation resource.
